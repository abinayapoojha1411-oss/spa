Frequently Inquired

Guest Etiquette & Questions

Do you accommodate walk-in appointments? ▼

While we always do our best to accommodate walk-in guests, our master artists and private suites are frequently reserved weeks in advance. We strongly recommend scheduling in advance online or via WhatsApp.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy? ▼

We understand schedules change. You may reschedule or cancel your appointment free of charge up to 2 hours prior to your scheduled time via WhatsApp or telephone.

Are your cosmetic products cruelty-free and organic? ▼

Yes, entirely. We strictly partner with clean luxury brands, including cruelty-free botanical hair dyes, 9-free non-toxic nail builder gels, and hypoallergenic Swiss-formulated skin actives.

Do you offer bridal party bookings and private salon hire? ▼

Yes, our penthouse suite can be booked exclusively for brides and bridal parties with custom grazing tables, French champagne, and dedicated senior artists. Contact us via the form above for custom bridal itineraries.
